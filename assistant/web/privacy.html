Privacy policy

Syllabuddy · last updated 4 October 2026

What Syllabuddy stores

To build your revision list, Syllabuddy keeps a small history for each student:

  • the syllabus objective a question matched (for example 9758.3.3) and the question's text, up to 500 characters;
  • quiz results: which objective was tested, whether the answer was right, and a short note on what was missing;
  • the time of each event.

History is keyed by an anonymous student id. When signed in through a voice assistant, that id is a one-way hash of the access token, never the token itself, your name, or your email.

What it does not store

No voice recordings, no names, no contact details, no location, and no payment information. Speech is turned into text before Syllabuddy sees it, by your voice assistant or by your browser's speech recognition (Chrome and Edge send the audio to their own cloud speech service; typing avoids that).

Who sees it

Only you, through tools like "what should I revise first?". History is stored in a database on the Syllabuddy server and is not sold, shared or used for advertising. Questions are sent to the language model that runs the conversation (for example Amazon Bedrock), under that provider's terms.

Deleting your history

Say "delete my Syllabuddy history" (the clear_my_history tool). Syllabuddy asks you to confirm, then removes everything stored for your id.

Children

Syllabuddy is a study aid for pre-university students. It collects no personal information beyond the anonymous history above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the project's repository.

What Syllabuddy is

A free study aid that answers questions about the published Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level syllabuses. Answers about what is examinable are taken from the syllabus documents, with the objective code and page so you can check them.

Check the official documents

Syllabuses change, and parsing documents can go wrong. Always confirm anything important with the current syllabus published by SEAB and with your teachers. Syllabuddy is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEAB, Cambridge, or Amazon.

Explanations

Explanations of concepts are generated by a language model and can contain mistakes. The syllabus facts (objective codes, included and excluded content) are not generated.

Acceptable use

Use Syllabuddy for your own studying. Don't attempt to disrupt the service or access other students' history.
